# MamaRise

> **Rise After Birth. Rise Into Everything Next.**

**Supporting Mothers Back to Work, Wellbeing, and Everyday Life After Birth.**

MamaRise is a digital companion designed to support mothers through the **first year after childbirth**, helping them navigate recovery, wellbeing, follow-up care, and the transition back into work or income-generating activities.

## 🎯 The Problem

After childbirth, much of the structured maternal support available during pregnancy disappears, even though mothers continue facing major challenges.

These include:

* Returning to work without adequate support or a clear plan
* Finding affordable and trustworthy childcare
* Managing breastfeeding and lactation
* Missing postpartum follow-up appointments
* Coping with postpartum depression or anxiety
* Accessing family planning services
* Recovering physically while balancing work and caregiving

The gap is particularly significant for mothers working in the informal and gig economy, who often lack structured maternity benefits, leave, and workplace support.

## 💡 The Solution

MamaRise provides a personalized postpartum companion based on **how a mother actually earns a living**, rather than applying a one-size-fits-all recovery timeline.

The platform's core differentiator is the **Return-to-Work Planner**, supported by lightweight wellbeing check-ins and appointment reminders.

### Core MVP Features

#### 1. Return-to-Work Planner

Mothers create personalized plans based on:

* Planned return date
* Type of work
* Breastfeeding preferences
* Childcare needs

Supported work types include:

* Formal employment
* Self-employment
* Gig work
* Farming
* Trading
* Remote work

The planner generates a **weekly preparation checklist**.

#### 2. Lightweight Wellbeing Check-In

A simple, low-friction log for:

* Mood
* Sleep
* Stress

The MVP deliberately does **not** provide clinical diagnosis or validated mental-health screening. Full validated screening is deferred until proper clinical review and an appropriate escalation pathway exist.

#### 3. Appointment & Milestone Reminders

MamaRise provides reminders for important postpartum milestones, including:

* Six-week postpartum check-up
* Three-month follow-up
* Six-month follow-up
* Family planning appointments

This feature is intended to provide high perceived value while remaining relatively simple to implement.

## 🚧 MVP Scope

MamaRise is intentionally focused on proving its core thesis within the July–December build window.

### Included in MVP

* Return-to-Work Planner
* Lightweight Wellbeing Check-In
* Appointment & Milestone Reminders

### Deferred to Phase 2

The following features are deliberately excluded from the initial MVP:

* Childcare Finder
* Full breastfeeding and lactation toolkit
* Emergency warning-sign symptom checker
* Full validated mental-health screening

These features require additional trust infrastructure, clinical review, legal consideration, or escalation pathways before they can be responsibly introduced.

## 🏆 Competitive Advantage

Most maternal health applications focus on:

* Pregnancy
* Antenatal care
* Delivery
* Baby growth

MamaRise instead focuses on the **mother after childbirth**, specifically her recovery, wellbeing, and return to economic activity.

The product therefore addresses an underserved stage of the maternal care journey rather than extending a conventional pregnancy tracker.

## ⚠️ Important Considerations

### Product Validation

The concept still requires validation with real postpartum mothers.

The project documentation proposes interviewing **15–20 mothers** through clinics, hospitals, or existing mother-support groups before committing fully to the product assumptions.

### Mental Health

Any wellbeing feature that identifies potential risk needs a real pathway to human support, such as:

* A hotline
* Clinic partner
* Counselor contact

A risk alert without somewhere to go is considered a liability rather than a feature.

### Childcare Verification

A childcare marketplace requires a credible verification process for providers. Simply labeling providers as "verified" without a real verification system is not considered sufficient.

### Data Privacy

Postpartum health and mental-wellbeing information is highly sensitive.

The project documentation identifies the need for:

* Encryption at rest
* Clear user consent
* Protection of individual-level data
* No sale or sharing of individual-level data
* Only anonymized aggregate insights, where appropriate

The project specifically notes the applicability of Kenya's Data Protection Act.

## 🎯 Vision

MamaRise is built around a simple idea:
